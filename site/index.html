02 / The target workflow

From an allocation
to a verified outcome.

This is the lifecycle we are connecting. The pieces exist at different stages; the automatic handoffs are still in development.

Provision capacity.

Choose our infrastructure, a managed provider, or a customer-owned cloud account.

Enroll and authorize.

Bind host identity to the control plane. Scope the work through policy and a validated lease.

Execute and observe.

Use the agent or execution shell to perform the allowed operation and capture its result.

Verify and retain evidence.

Check the resulting state and preserve the evidence needed to explain the change.
